2. Information We Collect and Use

2.1 Files and content you choose to process

Depending on the features you use, the App may access photos, videos, audio recordings, documents, or other files that you actively select or create to perform tasks such as scanning, editing, conversion, recording, or export.

When a feature operates entirely on your device, the content may remain stored locally. If a feature uses cloud recognition, synchronization, backup, or other remote processing, this policy must accurately disclose what content is uploaded, why it is processed, who receives it, where it is stored, and for how long. Do not describe the App as fully offline unless all relevant processing is actually performed on-device.

2.2 Device, usage, and diagnostic information

To support reliability, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service, the App and its enabled service providers may process information such as device model, operating system, app version, language, network status, IP address, crash reports, error logs, interaction events, and device identifiers when applicable. The exact information depends on the features and SDKs in use.

2.3 Advertising and measurement (if applicable)

If the App displays ads, advertising partners may process device information, ad impressions, clicks, IP addresses, and advertising identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping, attribution, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to their own privacy notices and applicable law.

Where Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework requires consent for tracking across other companies' apps or websites, we will request permission as required. If you decline, we will not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for tracking without permission. You may still see non-personalized ads.

2.4 Subscriptions and in-app purchases (if applicable)

If we offer subscriptions or in-app purchases, Apple processes payments through the App Store. To verify purchases, restore entitlements, and provide support, we may process transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase dates, and subscription status. We do not directly receive your full payment card details submitted to Apple.

2.5 Customer support and information you provide

If you contact us for assistance, we may receive the contact information, description of the issue, screenshots, and diagnostic materials you choose to share. We use this information to respond, resolve issues, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send unrelated sensitive personal information.

2.6 Legal bases for processing

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, we rely on your consent, the necessity of providing services you request, compliance with legal obligations, or another lawful basis. We will obtain separate or additional consent where legally required.

6. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, obtain a copy of, correct, supplement, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information; withdraw consent; request information about processing; or exercise other statutory rights.

  • Change device permissions: Manage access to the camera, photos, microphone, and other resources in iOS Settings.
  • Change tracking preferences: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete local content: Remove files in the App or uninstall it to remove data within the App's local container. Files exported outside the App may remain.
  • Manage subscriptions: Manage or cancel auto-renewing subscriptions in your Apple account's Subscriptions settings. Uninstalling the App typically does not cancel a subscription.
  • Submit a privacy request: Email the contact address in Section 10 and describe your request. We may verify your identity where appropriate and will respond within the period required by applicable law or explain if we cannot fulfill the request.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Certain records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations, in which case we will restrict processing as required.
